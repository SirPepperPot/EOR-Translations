What gets checked?

The program matches rows by number, verifies the workbook belongs to this export, restores placeholders and formatting, and skips blank, unchanged, or invalid translations. Download the review report for skipped rows. Google does not label the workbook reliably enough to verify the target language, so check the language on Google before translating.
